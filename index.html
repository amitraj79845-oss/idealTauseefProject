Welcome to Ideal Public School

Ideal Public School is a trusted CBSE-accredited institution in Nawada, Bihar, dedicated to providing quality education with a focus on holistic development. Since 2013, we have nurtured young minds through certified teachers, creative lessons, and a safe learning environment that encourages every child to grow with confidence.

Our school offers regular classes in spacious, well-ventilated classrooms, complemented by sports facilities that promote fitness and teamwork. We believe in making learning engaging through activity-based education, visual methods, and creative approaches that inspire curiosity and smart thinking.

At Ideal Public School, every student receives personalized attention from experienced educators who guide them with skill, care, and proven teaching practices. Our commitment to safety, discipline, and academic excellence ensures that your child builds a strong foundation for future success.

Teaching Your Child Some Good Manners

At Ideal Public School, we believe good manners are the foundation of respectful living. Through daily guidance and positive reinforcement, we teach children essential habits like saying "please" and "thank you," greeting others warmly, waiting for their turn, listening without interrupting, and showing kindness to everyone. These simple yet powerful manners help your child build confidence, empathy, and strong relationships with peers, teachers, and families — preparing them to grow into polite, respectful, and caring individuals both inside and outside the classroom.
